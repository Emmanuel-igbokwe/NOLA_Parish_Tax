Fund Balance & Operating Surplus — How They Connect: The Fund Balance is the school's cumulative savings account — the total of every prior year's surplus retained since opening. The operating surplus shown in Section 04 is this year's deposit into that account. A school running a deficit is making a withdrawal. A shrinking fund balance year-over-year is the earliest signal of fiscal distress, visible before any CSAF metric breaches its threshold.
